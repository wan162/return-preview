    The dance floor is not an escape.

    It's a shared moment. A musical journey. Movement translated into emotions. And statements turned into actions.

    Darkness. Appearances. Fake freedom. What once felt like a place to disappear, a place to fit in, to pretend, now becomes a place to feel present.

    RE:TURN is about going back. Back to the body. Back to the sound. Back to what feels real. Back to art made by and for humans. Before the image. Before the performance. Before the distractions. Before the artificial.

    Behind every night there is a person who put their sweat, tears and heart into it. Mistakes included, for everyone to see. That is the part a machine cannot replace.

    “Everyone needs to feel welcome, safe and free to express themselves. Respect the people around you, boundaries, and the environment you're in. Keep it real, human, and authentic.”

    Ettore Guerzoni (Free Your Ego), co-founder

    “A space where you can become yourself again and experience techno without prejudice, as an artist and as part of the audience.”

    Diego Zúñiga (Zutri), co-founder

    Awareness

    Zero tolerance. Consent is essential and can be withdrawn at any time.

    At RE:TURN, we are committed to creating a space where everyone feels safe, respected, and welcome. We have zero tolerance for any form of discrimination, racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, harassment, intimidation, unwanted physical contact, or any other behavior that makes another person feel unsafe or uncomfortable.

    Respect each other's boundaries. Consent is essential and can be withdrawn at any time.

    If at any point during the event you experience or witness a situation that makes you feel uncomfortable, unsafe, or disrespected, please approach any member of the RE:TURN or Matrix Club team, security, or bar staff. We are here to listen, support you, and help you handle the situation.

    Take care of yourself, take care of each other, and help us keep the dance floor a safe space for everyone.
